# Founder Story SaaS

A SaaS product that helps founders turn their real personal and business experiences into strategic storytelling content.

## Core Idea

Founders already have valuable stories.

They have:

- failures
- wins
- customer conversations
- difficult decisions
- lessons
- experiments
- unpopular opinions
- breakthroughs
- mistakes
- transformations

The problem is that most founders struggle to identify which experiences are worth turning into content and how those stories can build attention, authority, trust, and demand.

This product is designed to solve that problem.
