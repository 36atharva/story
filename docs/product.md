# Product Definition

## Working Name

Founder Story SaaS

The final product name has not been selected yet.

---

## Problem

Founders are constantly experiencing things that could become valuable content:

* Customer conversations
* Failures
* Wins
* Mistakes
* Experiments
* Lessons
* Opinions
* Difficult decisions
* Business breakthroughs
* Personal experiences

However, most founders struggle to identify which experiences are worth sharing and how to turn those experiences into compelling business content.

As a result, they often fall back on generic content such as:

* "5 tips for..."
* "3 ways to..."
* "Here's why you should..."
* Generic industry advice
* Generic motivational content

This makes their content less differentiated and less connected to their actual business experience.

---

## Product Promise

Help founders turn their real experiences into strategic storytelling content that builds:

* Attention
* Authority
* Trust
* Demand

---

## Core Product Insight

A founder's own experiences are one of their strongest sources of differentiated content.

The product should therefore start with what is actually happening inside the founder's life and business rather than starting with a blank AI content-generation prompt.

The core transformation is:

```text
Experience
    ↓
Story
    ↓
Insight
    ↓
Authority
    ↓
Demand
```

---

## Product Positioning

The product is **not** another generic AI social media writer.

The core value is identifying the content potential hidden inside a founder's real experiences.

The product should answer:

> "Something happened in my business. What is the valuable story, insight, opinion, or lesson hiding inside it?"

The AI should help the founder discover what is worth saying before helping them write it.

---

## Target Users

The initial target users are:

* SaaS founders
* Startup founders
* Agency owners
* Consultants
* Coaches
* Experts
* Service business owners

The ideal user:

* Has genuine expertise or business experience
* Wants to build a personal brand
* Wants to attract customers through content
* Has interesting experiences but struggles to turn them into content
* Wants differentiated content rather than generic AI-generated posts
* Publishes or wants to publish on X and/or LinkedIn

---

## Core Workflow

### 1. Founder Context

The founder provides information about themselves and their business.

Potential fields:

* Name
* Company
* Role
* Website
* What they sell
* Target customer
* Problem they solve
* Differentiator
* Expertise
* Desired positioning
* Topics they want to talk about
* Opinions
* Writing style
* Words or phrases to avoid

This context should influence every subsequent AI output.

---

### 2. Story

The founder submits a raw personal or business experience.

The story does not need to be polished.

Examples:

> We lost a $20k client because I realized we were pitching our service completely wrong.

> A customer told me something that completely changed how I think about our product.

> I spent three months building a feature nobody wanted.

> We increased conversions after removing half the sales process.

> I almost quit the business last year.

The product should encourage founders to submit real experiences rather than trying to write a perfect post upfront.

---

### 3. Story Analysis

The system analyzes the raw story and identifies its underlying content potential.

Potential analysis dimensions:

* Core story
* Narrative tension
* Emotional element
* Conflict
* Turning point
* Business lesson
* Founder authority
* Audience relevance
* Commercial relevance
* Contrarian elements
* Customer insight
* Possible opinions
* Possible content directions

The analysis should extract meaning from the experience rather than simply summarize it.

---

### 4. Content Opportunities

The system generates multiple content opportunities from the same story.

Each opportunity should represent a meaningfully different way of using the story.

Potential opportunity types:

* Founder story
* Contrarian opinion
* Business lesson
* Customer insight
* Failure story
* Transformation story
* Educational angle
* Industry observation
* Personal belief
* Demand-generation angle
* Conversion angle

For example, one experience could produce:

```text
Original experience
        ↓
"We lost a $20k client."
        ↓
 ┌───────────────────────┐
 │ Founder Story         │
 │ Sales Lesson          │
 │ Contrarian Opinion    │
 │ Customer Insight      │
 │ Demand Angle          │
 └───────────────────────┘
```

The product should show the founder that a single real experience can contain multiple pieces of valuable content.

---

## Content Objectives

Content opportunities should be classified according to their primary business objective.

### Reach

Designed to attract attention and maximize relevance or shareability.

### Authority

Demonstrates expertise, experience, and knowledge.

### Trust

Helps the audience understand the founder's personality, experiences, values, and decision-making.

### Demand

Connects the founder's experience to a problem their target customer has.

### Conversion

Creates a natural connection between the story, the customer's problem, and the founder's offer.

These categories are not performance guarantees.

The product should help founders choose the purpose of a piece of content rather than promising that a particular post will go viral or generate customers.

---

## 5. Content Generation

The founder selects a content opportunity.

The system then generates platform-specific content based on:

* Founder context
* Original story
* Story analysis
* Selected content opportunity
* Content objective
* Platform
* Founder writing style

Initial platforms:

* X
* LinkedIn

The generated content should preserve the founder's underlying experience and point of view.

It should not turn every story into generic motivational or educational content.

---

## 6. Save Content

The founder should be able to save useful generated content.

Saved content should retain its relationship to:

* Original story
* Story analysis
* Content opportunity
* Platform
* Generated output

This creates the foundation for a future founder content library.

---

# MVP Scope

The first version should allow a founder to:

1. Create an account.
2. Create a founder/business profile.
3. Store their positioning and content context.
4. Submit a raw personal or business story.
5. Analyze the story.
6. Receive multiple content opportunities.
7. Select an opportunity.
8. Generate X or LinkedIn content.
9. Save useful outputs.

The MVP should focus on proving the core workflow:

```text
Real Founder Experience
        ↓
Story
        ↓
Analysis
        ↓
Content Opportunities
        ↓
Selected Angle
        ↓
Useful Content
```

# AI Philosophy

The AI should not simply generate generic social media posts.

The intended intelligence flow is:

```text
Story
 ↓
Understand what happened
 ↓
Identify narrative
 ↓
Identify tension
 ↓
Identify insight
 ↓
Identify founder authority
 ↓
Identify audience relevance
 ↓
Identify business relevance
 ↓
Generate content opportunities
 ↓
Generate selected content
```

The product should prioritize **understanding before generation**.

The AI should be treated as a reasoning layer over founder experiences, not merely as a text-generation button.

---

# Founder Context as Memory

The founder profile should eventually become persistent context for the product.

Over time, the system may understand:

* Who the founder is
* What they sell
* Who they sell to
* Their expertise
* Their opinions
* Their positioning
* Their vocabulary
* Their experiences
* Their recurring themes
* Their previous stories
* Their published content

This creates the foundation for a potential future product concept:

> A Founder Narrative OS.

However, this broader system is **not required for the initial MVP**.

---

If the core workflow is validated, the product could eventually evolve beyond individual content generation.

Potential future capabilities:

* Persistent founder memory
* Large story library
* Story tagging
* Content history
* Content performance tracking
* Recommended stories to publish next
* Detection of recurring themes
* Founder positioning insights
* Audience insights
* Content performance feedback
* Content repurposing
* Newsletter generation
* Long-form content generation
* Multi-platform adaptation
* Publishing integrations
* Team workflows

These features should only be built after validating the core workflow.

---

# What Makes This Different

The product should not compete primarily on:

> "We generate better AI posts."

The differentiation should be:

> "We help you discover what is actually worth saying."

Generic AI content tools typically start with:

```text
Topic
→
Prompt
→
Post
```

This product should start with:

```text
Experience
→
Story
→
Meaning
→
Content Opportunity
→
Post
```

The founder's real experience is the source material.

AI is the interpretation and transformation layer.

---

# MVP Success Criteria

The MVP should demonstrate that:

1. A founder can onboard without assistance.
2. A founder can save their positioning and business context.
3. A founder can submit a raw story.
4. The system can identify useful storytelling angles.
5. At least one generated opportunity feels meaningfully different from generic AI content.
6. The founder can turn an opportunity into usable X or LinkedIn content.
7. The founder can save the result.
8. Founders understand the value without needing extensive explanation.
9. Founders are willing to submit additional stories.
10. At least some founders are willing to pay for the product.

---

# Initial Validation Goals

Initial validation targets:

* 20 design partners
* 10 active users
* 5 paying users
* Evidence that founders repeatedly submit stories
* Evidence that founders prefer story-driven outputs over generic content generation
* Evidence that the product saves meaningful time
* Evidence that founders would continue using the workflow

These are validation targets, not guarantees.

---

# Initial Business Model

The product should be monetized as early as practical.

Potential early pricing:

* ₹999/month
* ₹1,999/month
* ₹2,999/month

Alternatively:

* Discounted founding-member plan
* Limited founding cohort
* 3-month founding plan

Pricing is not final and should be validated with users.

The initial objective is not maximizing revenue.

The objective is proving that users will pay for the core value.

---

# Explicitly Out of Scope for Initial MVP

Do not build the following during the initial validation phase:

* Social media publishing
* X API integration
* LinkedIn API integration
* Social scheduling
* Social analytics
* CRM
* Lead scraping
* Automated outbound
* Team collaboration
* Mobile applications
* Browser extensions
* Voice input
* Image generation
* Video generation
* Autonomous agents
* Complex recommendation engines
* Advanced dashboards
* Gamification
* Subscription billing before product validation

These may be considered later based on actual user demand.

---

# Technical Philosophy

The MVP should remain technically simple.

Priorities:

1. Fast development
2. Low infrastructure cost
3. Simple architecture
4. Reliable AI outputs
5. Secure user data
6. Easy iteration
7. Easy deployment

Avoid premature infrastructure.

The initial product does not require:

* Microservices
* Vector databases
* Embeddings
* RAG
* Fine-tuning
* LangChain
* LangGraph
* Autonomous agents
* Complex event-driven architecture

These technologies may become useful later, but they are not necessary to validate the product.

---

# Core Product Principle

The product should always return to one fundamental idea:

> **Your best content already happened.**

The job of the product is to help founders recognize the value inside their experiences and turn that value into content that strengthens their personal brand and business.
